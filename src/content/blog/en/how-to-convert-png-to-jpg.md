## PNG or JPG: which one do you need?

Both are image formats, but they are built for different jobs.

- **PNG** is lossless. It keeps every pixel exactly, supports transparent backgrounds, and is best for screenshots, logos, icons and graphics with text.
- **JPG** (also written JPEG) is lossy. It removes detail you cannot really see, which makes files much smaller. It is best for photos, and almost every app, website and upload form accepts it.

So you usually want to convert PNG to JPG when a file is too big to send or upload, or when a form says "JPG only". A photo saved as PNG can easily be 5–10 MB; the same photo as a JPG is often under 1 MB with no visible difference. If you have the opposite problem, use [JPG to PNG](/jpg-to-png) instead.

## How to convert PNG to JPG on Windows

**With Paint:**

1. Right-click the PNG file and choose **Open with → Paint**.
2. Click **File → Save as → JPEG picture**.
3. Choose a name and folder, then click **Save**.

Paint may warn you that transparency will be lost. That is expected, because JPG has no transparent background.

**With the Photos app on Windows 11:** open the PNG, click the **See more** button (three dots), choose **Save as**, pick **.jpg** as the file type and click **Save**.

Both apps handle one file at a time. For a folder full of screenshots, the browser converter is much quicker.

## How to convert PNG to JPG on a Mac

**One file in Preview:**

1. Double-click the PNG to open it in **Preview**.
2. Click **File → Export**.
3. Set **Format** to **JPEG** and use the **Quality** slider (the middle-to-high end is fine for most images).
4. Click **Save**.

**Several files with a Quick Action:**

1. Select all the PNG files in Finder.
2. Right-click and choose **Quick Actions → Convert Image**.
3. Set **Format** to **JPEG**, pick an image size, and click **Convert to JPEG**.

The new JPGs appear next to the originals.

## How to convert PNG to JPG on iPhone

Screenshots on iPhone are saved as PNG, which is why they are often larger than your photos. There is no "Save as JPG" button in the Photos app, but you have two good options.

**In your browser:** open the [PNG to JPG converter](/png-to-jpg) in Safari, tap the box, choose your screenshots from the photo library, and tap **Download**. The JPGs are saved to the **Files** app, in Downloads.

**With the Shortcuts app:**

1. Open **Shortcuts** and tap **+** to make a new shortcut.
2. Add the action **Select Photos** and turn on **Select Multiple**.
3. Add **Convert Image** and set it to **JPEG**.
4. Add **Save to Photo Album**.
5. Run the shortcut, pick your PNGs, and the JPG copies appear in Photos.

## What happens to transparency when you convert PNG to JPG?

JPG has no transparent pixels, so every transparent area must be filled with a color. ConvertLime fills it with **white**, which looks right on most documents and web pages. Other apps may use white or black, so check a logo after converting.

If you need the background to stay see-through, keep the file as PNG. If you want to cut out a background first, use the [background remover](/remove-background) and save the result as PNG.

## Is the JPG still too big?

Most PNG screenshots shrink a lot when they become JPGs.
