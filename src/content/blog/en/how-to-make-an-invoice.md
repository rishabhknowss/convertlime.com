## What to include on an invoice

Every invoice should have these parts. Leaving one out is the most common reason an invoice gets paid late.

| Part | What to write | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your details | Business or full name, address, email, phone | Your client knows who to pay |
| Client details | Client name or company, address | Shows who owes the money |
| Invoice number | A unique number, e.g. INV-0001 | Needed for your records and theirs |
| Issue date | The day you send it | Starts the payment clock |
| Due date | The exact date payment is due | Removes any doubt about timing |
| Line items | Each product or service, quantity, price | Shows what the client is paying for |
| Totals | Subtotal, discount, tax, total due | The amount to pay, clearly worked out |
| Payment details | Bank details, payment link or method | Makes it easy to pay you |

Some countries ask for more, such as your VAT or GST number, or the client's tax number for business sales. If you are registered for tax, put your number under your business details.

## How to number your invoices

Every invoice needs a number that is unique and goes up in order. It helps you track who has paid, and many tax offices expect it. Simple formats work best:

- **INV-0001, INV-0002, INV-0003** – a prefix and a counter.
- **2026-001, 2026-002** – the year and a counter, restarting each year.
- **ACME-014** – a client code and a counter, if you bill a few clients a lot.

## How to add tax or VAT to an invoice

Tax is worked out on the subtotal after any discount. For example, two days of work at $400 a day is $800. With a 10% discount the subtotal becomes $720, and 20% VAT on that adds $144, so the total due is $864.

Write the tax rate on the invoice, not only the amount, so your client can check it. If you are not registered for tax, leave the rate at zero.

## Make your invoice look professional

A few small touches make an invoice look like it came from an established business:

- **Use your logo.** A logo with a transparent background looks best on a colored or dark template. If yours has a white box around it, use [remove background](/remove-background) to cut it out first.

- **Pick one accent color** that matches your brand and use it everywhere.
- **Write clear line descriptions**, such as "Website design – homepage and 3 pages" instead of "Work".

## When and how to send an invoice

Send the invoice as soon as the work is done or the goods are delivered; the sooner it goes out, the sooner you are paid. Attach the PDF to an email with a short message that gives the invoice number, the amount and the due date. Keep a copy of every invoice you send, and if the due date passes, send a polite reminder with the same invoice attached.
