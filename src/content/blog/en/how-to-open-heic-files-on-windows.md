Wondering how to open a HEIC file on your PC? On Windows 10 and 11, the Photos app can open a HEIC file once you add two small extensions from the Microsoft Store: **HEIF Image Extensions** (free) and **HEVC Video Extensions** (about $0.99). If you'd rather not install anything, convert the photo to JPG with the free [ConvertLime HEIC to JPG converter](/heic-to-jpg) – it then opens in every app.

## Why HEIC files won't open on Windows

HEIC is the photo format iPhones and iPads use. The picture inside is squeezed with a method called HEVC, which Microsoft doesn't include in Windows for free. So when you double-click a HEIC file, you may see:

- A blank or grey icon in File Explorer
- "We can't open this file" in the Photos app
- A prompt asking you to get an extension from the Microsoft Store

The photo itself is fine. Windows just doesn't have the right add-on yet. For the background, see [what a HEIC file is](/blog/what-is-a-heic-file).

## How to open a HEIC file with the Windows extensions

1. Open the **Microsoft Store** app (search for "Store" in the Start menu).
2. Search for **HEIF Image Extensions**. If it says **Install**, install it. It's free and often already there.
3. Search for **HEVC Video Extensions** from Microsoft. Buy and install it – it usually costs about $0.99.
4. Close the Photos app if it's open, then double-click your HEIC file.

The photo should now open in **Photos**. File Explorer will also start showing thumbnails, and **Paint** can open the photos too.

Look closely before you pay: some laptops come with **HEVC Video Extensions from Device Manufacturer** already installed, and that version works just as well.

## Stop receiving HEIC files

If the photos come from your own iPhone:

1. On the iPhone, open **Settings → Photos** and set **Transfer to Mac or PC** to **Automatic**. Photos copied to your PC by cable then arrive as JPG.
2. To make the iPhone take JPG photos, go to **Settings → Camera → Formats** and choose **Most Compatible**.

If the photos come from someone else, ask them to email the photos (iPhones usually send JPGs by email) or share the converter link with them. Not sure which format is better to keep? Our [HEIC vs JPEG](/blog/heic-vs-jpeg) guide compares them.

## Still won't open?

**The extensions are installed but Photos still shows an error.** Restart your PC, then try again. Also check Windows Update for Store app updates.

**The file ends in .HEIC but is tiny or 0 KB.** The download or copy may have failed. Copy it from the phone or cloud again.

**It's a .MOV file, not a photo.** That's the motion part of a Live Photo. It's a short video, and the matching .HEIC file is the still picture.
