## Why forms ask for such a small file

Online application forms receive lakhs of photos and signatures. Small files keep the website fast and the records easy to store and print on admit cards. That is why many forms ask for:

- A **JPG or JPEG** file (not PNG, not HEIC, not PDF)
- A **maximum file size**, often a small number of KB such as 20KB or 50KB
- Sometimes a **minimum file size** too, so the photo is not too blurry
- Sometimes exact **pixel sizes** (width × height) or a ratio

These rules are different for every exam and can change every year. **Read the official notice for your form** and write down the exact numbers before you start.

## How to resize an image to 20KB with ConvertLime

1. **Crop first.** For a photo, keep your head and the top of your shoulders. For a signature, cut away the empty white paper around it. Your phone's Photos or Gallery app has a crop button under Edit.
2. Open the [Resize Image to 20KB tool](/resize-image-to-20kb) in any browser – Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
3. Drop your photo into the box, or tap it to choose a file from your phone or computer.
4. The tool lowers the JPG quality a little at a time until the file fits under 20KB. If the photo is very large, it also shrinks the width and height.
5. Tap **Download**. The new file is a JPG, ready to upload.

## Photo or signature: what to check before uploading

| | Photo | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Plain, light colour (often white) | Plain white paper |
| What to crop to | Face and top of shoulders | The signature only, small margin |
| Common mistake | Full-body photo, face too small | Blue lines from ruled paper |
| Looks fine at 20KB? | Yes, if cropped tightly | Yes, very easily |
| File type | JPG / JPEG | JPG / JPEG |

A signature has only dark lines on white, so it shrinks to 20KB with almost no visible change. A photo has more detail, so cropping close to the face matters more.

## Tips to keep your photo clear at 20KB

- **Use good light.** Stand facing a window during the day. A dark, grainy photo turns muddy when it is made small.
- **Start with the original photo,** not a screenshot or a WhatsApp forward. WhatsApp already squeezes photos, and squeezing twice looks worse.
- **Keep the face large in the frame.** A tight crop gives the face more of the 20KB to use.
- **Do not add filters or beauty effects.** Many forms say the photo must look natural and recent.
- **Check the result on a big screen** if you can. Zoom in on the eyes – if they are clear, the photo is fine.

## How to make an image 20KB without a website

You can do it with the apps already on your computer, but it takes trial and error, because they let you choose pixels or quality, not an exact KB number.

**On Windows:** open the photo in **Paint**, click **Resize**, choose **Pixels**, and type a smaller width (for example 300). Then use **File → Save as → JPEG picture**. Right-click the saved file and choose **Properties** to see the size. If it is still over 20KB, make it smaller again.

**On a Mac:** open the photo in **Preview**, choose **Tools → Adjust Size** to lower the width, then **File → Export**, pick **JPEG** and drag the **Quality** slider to the left. Preview shows the file size below the slider, so you can stop once it reads under 20KB.

**On a phone:** most phones do not have a built-in "make this 20KB" option, which is why a browser tool is the quickest way.

## Common upload errors and what they mean

- **"File size exceeds limit"** – the file is bigger than allowed. Run it through the 20KB tool again or pick a smaller limit.
- **"File size is less than minimum"** – the file is too small. Use a clearer original or the [compressor](/compress-image) at a higher quality.
- **"Invalid file format"** – the form wants JPG. Photos from the tool are always JPG; make sure the name ends in `.jpg` or `.jpeg`.
- **"Image dimensions are not valid"** – the form wants exact pixel sizes. Crop or resize to those numbers first, then make it 20KB.

Once your photo and signature both upload, take a screenshot of the preview page before you pay the fee or submit, so you have a record of what you sent.
