Here is how to save WebP as JPG: save the WebP image as you normally would, then convert it to JPG, either with a free online converter or with a program already on your computer such as Paint on Windows or Preview on a Mac. Browsers do not let you choose JPG when saving a picture, because they save whatever file the website sent, and these days that is often WebP. Below are the steps for every device, starting with the fastest.

## The fastest way: save, then convert in your browser

This works the same on Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android and Chromebook.

1. Right-click the picture on the website (or press and hold on a phone) and save it. It will save as `.webp`.
2. Open the free [WebP to JPG converter](/webp-to-jpg).
3. Drop the WebP file into the box, or tap it to choose the file.
4. Click **Download**. You now have a normal `.jpg` file.

The conversion happens inside your browser, so the picture is not sent to anyone. If you saved several images, add them all at once and download them as a ZIP.

## How to save WebP as JPG on Windows

Windows 11 can open WebP images in Paint, so you can save a JPG copy without installing anything.

1. Right-click the `.webp` file and choose **Open with → Paint**.
2. In Paint, click **File → Save as**.
3. Choose **JPEG picture**.
4. Pick a folder, give it a name, and click **Save**.

Paint handles one picture at a time, so for more than a few files the online converter is quicker. If the image had a transparent background, Paint fills it with a solid colour.

## How to save WebP as JPG on a Mac

Preview, the Mac's built-in viewer, can export to JPG.

1. Double-click the `.webp` file to open it in **Preview**.
2. Click **File → Export**.
3. Set **Format** to **JPEG** and drag the quality slider towards **Best**.
4. Click **Save**.

To do several at once in Preview, select all the files in Finder, open them together, select every thumbnail in the sidebar, and use **File → Export Selected Images**.

## How to save WebP as JPG on iPhone or iPad

Recent versions of iOS can show WebP images in Photos and Files, but there is no simple "save as JPG" button. The easiest options:

- Open the [WebP to JPG converter](/webp-to-jpg) in Safari, tap the box, choose the image from **Photos** or **Files**, and tap **Download**.
- Or take a screenshot of the picture. This is quick, but the image will include only what was on your screen, at your screen's size.

## How to save WebP as JPG on Android

Android phones open WebP in most gallery apps, but menus differ between brands, so not every phone has a built-in way to save as JPG. The reliable route is the same as above: open the converter in Chrome, tap the box, pick the WebP image, and download the JPG. It ends up in your **Downloads** folder.

## Why not just rename the file?

It is tempting to change `.webp` to `.jpg` and move on. Unfortunately the picture inside is still WebP. Some apps will open it anyway, but many upload forms check what is inside the file and reject it, and some programs will say the file is damaged. A real conversion is the only way to get a true JPG.

## JPG or PNG?

JPG is the right choice for photos, product pictures and anything you want to upload, email or print. Choose PNG when the image has a see-through background, sharp text or a logo. For that, follow our guide on [how to convert WebP to PNG](/blog/how-to-convert-webp-to-png) or go straight to the [WebP to PNG converter](/webp-to-png).

A JPG is often larger than the WebP it came from.
