What is a HEIC file? It is a photo saved in the **High Efficiency Image Container** format. It has been the default on iPhones and iPads since iOS 11, so if a photo came from an iPhone and ends in `.heic`, that is why. HEIC photos look just as good as JPGs at about half the size – the problem is that Windows, many websites and older apps cannot open them.

The quickest fix is to convert the photo to JPG, which opens everywhere.

## Why do iPhones use HEIC?

Apple switched to HEIC to save storage. A typical 12-megapixel iPhone photo is around 1.5–2 MB as HEIC versus 3–4 MB as JPG, with no visible difference. HEIC can also store several images in one file, which is how Live Photos and burst shots work.

## Why won't my HEIC file open?

HEIC uses the HEVC (H.265) codec, which is patent-licensed. That is why support is patchy:

| Where you open it | Does HEIC work? |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone, iPad, Mac (Photos, Preview) | Yes |
| Windows 10 / 11 Photos app | Only with the paid HEVC extension |
| Chrome, Edge, Firefox | No |
| Google Photos, Dropbox | Yes, they show a preview |
| Job, visa and exam upload forms | Usually rejected |
| Word, PowerPoint, older Photoshop | Often no |

## How to open a HEIC file without converting

- **On Windows:** install *HEVC Video Extensions* from the Microsoft Store (it costs about $0.99), then the Photos app will open HEIC.
- **On Mac:** double-click the file – Preview opens it natively.
- **On Android:** Google Photos and most recent gallery apps can display HEIC.

If you only need to send or upload the photo, converting is faster than installing anything.

## How to stop your iPhone taking HEIC photos

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Camera**, then **Formats**.
3. Choose **Most Compatible**.

New photos will now save as JPG. Your iPhone also converts HEIC to JPG automatically when you email photos or AirDrop them to a non-Apple device – set **Settings → Photos → Transfer to Mac or PC** to **Automatic**.

## Can I just rename .heic to .jpg?

No. Renaming changes only the label, not the data inside. Some apps will refuse the file, others will show an error or a blank image. A real conversion decodes the HEIC photo and saves it again as a proper JPEG, which is what the converter above does.

## Will converting change my photo?

The picture itself stays the same: same colours, same size in pixels, same orientation. Two things are worth knowing:

- **File size goes up.** A JPG of the same photo is usually 1.5–2× larger than the HEIC. If a form has a size limit, [resize the JPG to 100KB](/resize-image-to-100kb) or [50KB](/resize-image-to-50kb) after converting.
- **Live Photo motion is dropped.** A Live Photo is a still HEIC plus a short video. Converting keeps the still frame.

## How to send HEIC photos to someone on Windows or Android

If you are sharing from an iPhone, the simplest options are:

1. **Email or messaging apps** – iOS usually converts the photo to JPG automatically when it leaves the Photos app.
2. **AirDrop to a Mac** – the Mac keeps HEIC and opens it fine.
3. **USB cable to a PC** – set *Transfer to Mac or PC* to *Automatic* so Windows receives JPGs.
4. **Cloud links** (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive) – these keep the original HEIC, so the person receiving them may need to convert. Send them the [HEIC to JPG converter](/heic-to-jpg) link; it works on their phone too.

| | HEIC | JPG |
