To convert JPG to PDF, open a free tool such as ConvertLime's [JPG to PDF converter](/jpg-to-pdf), add your photos, pick a page size, and download the PDF. That is the quickest way on any device. You can also do it with tools already on your computer or phone: **Microsoft Print to PDF** on Windows, **Preview** on Mac, and the **Files** or **Print** options on iPhone. This guide shows how to convert JPG to PDF with each one, step by step.

## How to convert JPG to PDF with ConvertLime (any device)

1. Open the [JPG to PDF converter](/jpg-to-pdf) in your browser.
2. Drop your JPG files into the box, or tap to choose them. Add them in the order you want the pages to appear.
3. Pick a page size:
   - **Same as image** – each page is the exact shape of the photo, with no white edges.
   - **A4** – standard paper size, best for printing and for official forms.
4. Click **Download**. Each photo is on its own page in one PDF.

Your files stay on your device the whole time. If you have PNG files, use [PNG to PDF](/png-to-pdf); for a mix of formats, use [Image to PDF](/image-to-pdf).

## How to convert JPG to PDF on Windows

Windows 10 and 11 come with a built-in "printer" called **Microsoft Print to PDF** that saves to a PDF file instead of paper.

1. Open **File Explorer** and find your JPG files. Select one, or hold **Ctrl** and click to select several.
2. Right-click and choose **Print**. On Windows 11 you may need to click **Show more options** first.
3. In the **Print Pictures** window, set the printer to **Microsoft Print to PDF**.
4. Choose a paper size and a layout on the right (for example **Full page photo**).
5. If you do not want your photo cropped, untick **Fit picture to frame**.
6. Click **Print**, choose where to save the file, type a name, and click **Save**.

You can also open a single photo in the **Photos** app, press **Ctrl + P**, and choose Microsoft Print to PDF as the printer.

## How to convert JPG to PDF on a Mac

**One photo:**

1. Double-click the JPG to open it in **Preview**.
2. Click **File → Export as PDF**.
3. Choose a name and location, then click **Save**.

**Several photos in one PDF:**

1. Select all the JPGs in Finder, then right-click and choose **Open With → Preview**.
2. In the sidebar on the left, drag the pictures into the order you want.
3. Click **File → Print**.
4. At the bottom left, open the **PDF** menu and choose **Save as PDF**.

## How to convert JPG to PDF on iPhone

The easiest built-in way uses the **Files** app:

1. In **Photos**, select your pictures, tap **Share**, and choose **Save to Files**.
2. Open the **Files** app and go to the folder you saved them in.
3. Tap **Select** (or the **More** button, then **Select**) and choose the photos.
4. Tap the **More** button (three dots) at the bottom right and choose **Create PDF**.

The new PDF appears in the same folder. iPhone photos are often HEIC rather than JPG; if you want to turn them straight into a PDF in your browser, use [HEIC to PDF](/heic-to-pdf). Our guide to [what a HEIC file is](/blog/what-is-a-heic-file) explains the difference.

## How to convert JPG to PDF on Android

Most Android phones can print to PDF:

1. Open the photo in **Google Photos** or your gallery app.
2. Tap the **More** menu (three dots) and look for **Print**.
3. At the top of the print screen, choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.
4. Tap the PDF download button and choose where to save.

Menu names vary between phone makers. If you do not see Print, or you want several photos in one PDF, the [JPG to PDF converter](/jpg-to-pdf) works in Chrome on Android.

## Is the PDF too big to send?

A PDF made from phone photos can be several MB. If an email or form rejects it, make the photos lighter first with the [image compressor](/compress-image), then turn them into a PDF.
